# Industrial Digital Twins Technical Committee

## Overview

The Digital Twins Technical Committee has been initiated under the aegis of the French Solutions Industrie du Futur initiative, piloted by CORI2DF in collaboration with CEA List and Dassault Systèmes. Since late 2020, it brings together a significant panel of industrial and academic actors to advance the topic of digital twins. It is now sponsored by the EDT program and its activity fall under the AT5 (Cross-Cuting Action 5 : Dissemination, Exploitation, Standardization, Education)

A Digital Twin is an organized set of digital models representing a real-world entity to address specific problems and use cases. It is kept up to date with the real entity, at a frequency and precision suited to its use cases, and equipped with advanced exploitation tools to understand, analyze, predict, and optimize the operation and control of the real entity.

Coordination: Olivier Scart (3DS) - Pascale Vicat-Blanc (Inria).

## Objectives

The committee's first objective was to explain what a Digital Twin is and the benefits it can bring to industrial companies — in particular SMEs — which led to a first deliverable (see Brochures below).

Following the publication of that first brochure, the committee has undertaken to:

* Promote the Digital Twin across the French and European industrial fabric through information brochures and participation in events (conferences, trade shows, colloquia).
* Foster exchanges with European counterparts to create synergies, such as with the International Digital Twin Association (IDTA).

The committee has then continued its work towards a new publication on the technical implementation of the Digital Twin, exploring its functional architecture, the associated challenges, and its governance.
